The ultimate 4.8.X intel tool for the elite Star Citizen miner. Currently updated to be in line with 4.8.3 changes.

The STAR CITIZEN MINING MATERIAL FINDER 2.0 has been completely rebuilt and is updated for 4.8.3 from the ground up for future additions. I've dropped reliance on third-party APIs — instead it's now powered by a custom compiler that reads raw CIG game data and produces a light file, making the app incredibly light and fast. Works great on Desktop & Mobile.

It eliminates spreadsheet hunting, giving you 100% accurate drop rates and locations so you can mine more effectively — and now it doesn't stop once you've found the rock. From there it helps you gear up for it, refine it, and go buy exactly what you're missing. Whether you're looking for direction during an event, want to maximize your crafted gear's quality, or need a full loadout built for you in under a minute, this tool gives you everything you need to mine smarter. It keeps things simple so new or old players alike can jump into Star Citizen 4.8 and start mining with relative ease.

🕹️ LOCATION DATABASE: Choose Stanton, Pyro, or Nyx. Select up to 3 required materials (Quantainium, Laranite, etc.). The tool will instantly filter the database and display the locations' mathematical drop-percentage chances for Ship, ROC, and Hand mining deposits. Lagrange pools with shared mining values, like ARC-L5, CRU-L4, MIC-L3, are smartly grouped together to save you time.

🚀 HUD RS SCANNER: Enter the raw RS signature reading straight from your ship's HUD. The tool uses exact cluster math, instantly telling you the exact material, the rarity tier, the mining method required, and how many nodes make up that specific cluster before you reach it.

⛏️ LOADOUT & GEAR — NEW: A full mining loadout calculator, merged right into the same tool. Pick your ship — Prospector, MOLE, or Golem — and every screen restricts itself to gear that actually fits it. Three ways to build:

Beginner Wizard: Allows new players to pick from one of 3 profiles for each of the 3 primary miner ships in the game to quickly add to the cart, and go purchase the parts to build with intelligent route planning and part count. 

Advanced Min-Max: choose up to two stats to maximize per turret — Laser Power, Resistance, Optimal Window, or Instability Reduction — and the optimizer finds the best head/module combo for you, with weakness warnings with gadget reccomendations if your build is pushing boundaries of what will work reliably.

Manual Builder: pick every laser, module, and gadget yourself from searchable dropdowns that show live stats as you scroll — including a colored badge naming exactly which component is boosting or hurting each stat and by how much, so you can compare parts without guessing.

🛒 CART & BUY ROUTE — NEW: Any build you land on — wizard, optimizer, or manual — can be carted in one click, quantities and all. Set your current system and planet, and the tool builds the shortest shopping route across the fewest vendor stops, sequenced nearest-next, with a running aUEC total for the whole haul.

⚗️ REFINING METHODS — NEW: All nine in-game refining methods laid out side by side — Efficient, Normal, or Wasteful — with speed, cost, and yield for each, plus a beginner quick-pick showing the single best-value method in every category.

⚠️ PRO TIP & ADVISORY: I highly advise focusing your mining operations in Pyro, as the material is far higher quality on average than what is present in Stanton. Note: Sadaryx & Savrilium can be found specifically at the Rock Breaker Contract Locations.
