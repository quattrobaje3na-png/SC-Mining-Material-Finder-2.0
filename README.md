The ultimate 4.10.X intel tool for the elite Star Citizen miner. 

Currently updated to be in line with 4.10 changes.

The STAR CITIZEN MINING MATERIAL FINDER 2.0 has been completely rebuilt and is updated for 4.9 from the ground up with new features. 

It eliminates spreadsheet hunting by giving you 100% accurate drop rates and locations so you can mine more effectively. This new version doesn't stop once you've found the rock. The tool helps you prep before you even start looking for rocks: gear up, map your shopping route to upgrade your miner, and have access to the essentials of refining you need. Whether you are looking for direction during an event, maximizing your crafted gear's quality, or building a full loadout in under a minute, you get everything you need to mine smarter in one tool. It keeps the process simple so both new players and veterans can jump into Star Citizen 4.8.3 and start mining with ease.

🕹️ LOCATION DATABASE: Choose Stanton, Pyro, or Nyx. Select up to 3 required materials (Quantainium, Laranite, etc.). The tool will instantly filter the database and display the locations' mathematical drop percentage chances for Ship, ROC, and Hand mining deposits. Lagrange pools with shared mining values, like ARC-L5, CRU-L4, MIC-L3, are smartly grouped together to save you time and headaches.

🚀 HUD RS SCANNER: Enter the raw RS signature reading straight from your ship's HUD. The tool uses exact cluster math, instantly telling you the exact material, the rarity tier, the mining method required, and how many nodes make up that specific cluster before you reach it.

⛏️ LOADOUT & GEAR: A full mining loadout calculator, merged right into the same tool. Choose either a Prospector, Mole, or Golem and the tool tells you what to equip. On top of this every ship selection restricts itself to gear that actually fits it. This part of the tool has 3 different modes. 

Beginner Wizard: Allows new players to pick from one of 3 profiles for each of the 3 primary miner ships in the game to quickly add to the cart, and go purchase in game with intelligent route planning and part count.
Advanced Min-Max: Choose up to two stats to maximize per turret Laser Power, Resistance, Optimal Window, or Instability Reduction. The optimizer finds the best head & module combo for you, with weakness warnings with gadget recommendations if your build is pushing boundaries of what will work reliably.

Manual Builder: Pick every laser, module, and gadget yourself from searchable dropdowns that show live stats as you scroll. This section includes easy to read colored badges that say exactly which component is boosting or hurting each stat and by how much, so you can compare parts without guessing.

🛒 CART & BUY ROUTE: Any build you land on wizard, optimizer, or manual can be carted in one click, quantities and all. Set your current system and planet, and the tool builds the shortest shopping route across the fewest vendor stops, sequenced nearest-next, with a running aUEC total for the whole haul.

⚗️ REFINING METHODS: The tool now includes a quick reference Refining chart.

⚠️ PRO TIP & ADVISORY: I highly advise focusing your mining operations in Pyro, as the material is far higher quality on average than what is present in Stanton. Note: Sadaryx & Savrilium can be found specifically at the Rock Breaker Contract Locations.
